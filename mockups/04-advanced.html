[ 06 ] Straight answers

Before you ask

A marketing site with real content: four to seven weeks from kickoff. A platform or dashboard: eight to sixteen, depending on how much of the backend already exists. We give you a date after discovery, not before — a number quoted off a one-paragraph brief is a guess wearing a suit.

Priced per project after a paid discovery week, so both sides are working from the same picture. Retainers are monthly. We will tell you early if your budget and your brief are not in the same room — that conversation is cheaper now than in month three.

Yes, and often that is the better arrangement. We can take design only, build only, or slot alongside in-house engineers in your repo and your board. We will not pretend a takeover is necessary when an embed would serve you better.

You do, from the first commit. Repositories, infrastructure and credentials are in your accounts, not ours. Handover is a documented step in the process, not a negotiation at the end of it.

Your choice. Some clients take the keys and run; some keep a retainer for iteration and monitoring. Either way you leave with documentation a new developer can actually use, which is the part most agencies quietly skip.
